# Parcel Pack

Native SwiftUI puzzle for iPhone, iOS 17+, also playable on macOS. Two modes share the cardboard board, parcel rendering, placement helpers, and visual system. Parcels stay as shown: no player rotation, including hints and score searches. Each generated reference fill uses those exact orientations. **Shift** makes parcel value, shelf choices, and rush deadlines matter across a five-box batch. **Free pack** preserves the original untimed exact-fill puzzle and hints. DESIGN_VARIANCE=6 MOTION_INTENSITY=3 VISUAL_DENSITY=3.

Signature: a warm cardboard grid filled with rounded, saturated parcel tiles, each carrying a small tape seam. SF Rounded provides friendly native numerals; supporting copy uses native text. Type scale: 14/16/20/28/36 points. Four-point spacing base, 12- and 24-point container radii; tile corners scale with cell size. One soft shadow appears on lifted parcels.

Palette roles (sRGB equivalents for SwiftUI): background #F8F4EC, surface #FFFCF6, foreground #253D38, muted #5D7068, cardboard #E3D4BD, grid #D1BEA0, accent #267B61, accent foreground #FFFFFF. Parcel fills #D98765, #E5B74C, #70A69C, #8F9FC3, #A0AE77, #CB8EAD. Dark foreground on parcel fills. Invalid placement uses a red outline plus unavailable icon, not color alone. The root uses a light color scheme so native mode controls remain legible with this fixed palette.

The board is the focal point in a portrait layout. The top mode picker switches between separately saved sessions. Shift shows the current shift, box, target score, and best cleared-shift record. Parcels display their value badge and a Rush/Due now label when applicable. The shelf has three visible slots above the tray. Undo, Shelve/Unshelve, and Seal stay at the bottom safe area while board and tray content can scroll. Board cells are at least 44 points at the supported 375-point width. Free pack retains its size picker, shipped count, and Undo/Hint controls.

Select-and-tap is a full alternative to dragging, including VoiceOver labels and actions. Dragging lifts the parcel above the finger; the ghost and final placement use the same rectangle. Respect Reduce Motion and support enlarged text with scrolling and a three-column tray at accessibility sizes. Phone accessibility behavior still needs device verification.

Sealing shows a shipped stamp and the actual packed value, full-box multiplier, returns, and late fees. The best-full-box note compares gross packing value before late fees and only appears when the solver proves the optimum. Five boxes resolve to a cleared shift or failed run; continuing generates another batch. Rush deadlines advance only when boxes ship. The shelf clears between shifts. No real-time countdown, idle income, permanent power upgrades, ads, lives, or online dependency.

The original Free pack generator remains separate from Shift's connected-region generator. Shift plans store a reference tiling, while the value solver uses branch-and-bound and an area knapsack bound to find the highest-value exact fill from a subset. Session and record saves are validated independently, so a rejected box cannot erase lifetime progress. Legacy packing boards migrate to reference orientations while retaining compatible footprints; incompatible pieces return to the tray. Scores, quotas, shelf IDs, and completion credits remain intact. Previously shipped Shift snapshots keep their original geometry until the next packing box starts. See the Swift sources and VALIDATION.md for measured behavior and limits.

Native Mac renders and interactions were checked at 430- and 375-point widths on October 12, 2026. The fixed-orientation iOS build is installed and running in Xcode 27 Device Hub on an iPhone 18 Pro simulator. Physical iPhone checks still require device signing and installation.
