# Parcel Pack

Native SwiftUI packing game for iPhone, iOS 17+, also playable on macOS. Three modes share the cardboard board, parcel rendering, placement helpers, and visual system. Parcels stay as shown: no player rotation, including hints and score searches. Each generated reference fill uses those exact orientations. **Tycoon** turns packing into cash, equipment, customers, and depot growth. **Shift** makes parcel value, shelf choices, and rush deadlines matter across a five-box batch. **Free pack** preserves the original untimed exact-fill puzzle and hints. DESIGN_VARIANCE=6 MOTION_INTENSITY=3 VISUAL_DENSITY=3.

Signature: a warm cardboard grid filled with rounded, saturated parcel tiles, each carrying a small tape seam. SF Rounded provides friendly native numerals; supporting copy uses native text. Type scale: 14/16/20/28/36 points. Four-point spacing base, 12- and 24-point container radii; tile corners scale with cell size. One soft shadow appears on lifted parcels.

Palette roles (sRGB equivalents for SwiftUI): background #F8F4EC, surface #FFFCF6, foreground #253D38, muted #5D7068, cardboard #E3D4BD, grid #D1BEA0, accent #267B61, accent foreground #FFFFFF. Parcel fills #D98765, #E5B74C, #70A69C, #8F9FC3, #A0AE77, #CB8EAD. Dark foreground on parcel fills. Invalid placement uses a red outline plus unavailable icon, not color alone. The root uses a light color scheme so native mode controls remain legible with this fixed palette.

The board is the focal point in a portrait layout. The top mode picker switches between separately saved sessions. Shift shows the current shift, box, target score, and best cleared-shift record. Parcels display their value badge and a Rush/Due now label when applicable. The shelf has three visible slots above the tray. Undo, Shelve/Unshelve, and Seal stay at the bottom safe area while board and tray content can scroll. Board cells are at least 44 points at the supported 375-point width. Free pack retains its size picker, shipped count, and Undo/Hint controls.

Tycoon uses bottom **Pack / Orders / Depot** tabs. Pack keeps the box central, with the customer's ticket and projected payment above it and Undo/Hint/Ship anchored below. A paid box shows its historical receipt even after an equipment purchase. Orders offers three deterministic boxes and a customer catalog with explicit building and delivery requirements. Depot uses a native isometric Canvas: walls, racks, desks, stock, and a delivery van reflect actual saved purchases. Five building stages visibly change the room. Affordable upgrades show their exact price and payout benefit; unavailable purchases explain the remaining cash or deliveries. Switching tabs starts at the top, and a partial order change requires confirmation.

Tycoon has eight customers and three equipment lines, each with 12 levels. Workbenches and shelves add $8 and $4 per level to base pay; vans add 10% per level to that subtotal. No-hint shipping adds 20% of the customer's base pay. Hinting remains free and never blocks progression. Expansion gates are $650/7 deliveries, $2,500/25, $9,000/65, and $25,000/130. New generated orders continue after the final building; staff and idle income are outside this first pass.

Select-and-tap is a full alternative to dragging, including VoiceOver labels and actions. Dragging lifts the parcel above the finger; the ghost and final placement use the same rectangle. Respect Reduce Motion and support enlarged text with scrolling and a three-column tray at accessibility sizes. Phone accessibility behavior still needs device verification.

Sealing in Shift shows a shipped stamp and the actual packed value, full-box multiplier, returns, and late fees. The best-full-box note compares gross packing value before late fees and only appears when the solver proves the optimum. Five boxes resolve to a cleared shift or failed run; continuing generates another batch. Rush deadlines advance only when boxes ship. The shelf clears between shifts. No real-time countdown, idle income, ads, lives, real-money purchases, or online dependency.

The original Free pack generator remains separate from Shift's connected-region generator. Shift plans store a reference tiling, while the value solver uses branch-and-bound and an area knapsack bound to find the highest-value exact fill from a subset. Session and record saves are validated independently, so a rejected box cannot erase lifetime progress. Legacy packing boards migrate to reference orientations while retaining compatible footprints; incompatible pieces return to the tray. Scores, quotas, shelf IDs, and completion credits remain intact. Previously shipped Shift snapshots keep their original geometry until the next packing box starts. See the Swift sources and VALIDATION.md for measured behavior and limits.

Tycoon reuses the exact-fill generator. A canonical order combines account seed, shipment sequence, offer choice, and customer. The business and active box are one validated snapshot; shipping saves the new cash and paid receipt before publishing the change to the interface. Relaunch cannot repay a shipment. The decoder can discard a malformed nested board while retaining a valid business. Legacy mode keys are unchanged, and Tycoon has its own save key.

Native Mac renders and interactions were checked at 430- and 375-point widths on October 12, 2026. Additional gesture, accessibility, audio, and haptic checks remain.
